How it works

A little effort goes a long way

ShareCare keeps the donation process straightforward.

👤

Create an account

Register as a donor or beneficiary and add your basic contact and location details.

📦

List or request

Donors add item details and a photo. Beneficiaries browse the listings and submit a request.

🤝

Review and hand over

The donor reviews requests, updates the status and coordinates the handover with the recipient.

Prototype note: online payments, live chat, advanced AI matching, delivery tracking and automatic pickup scheduling are not included. This demo stores information in this browser only.
